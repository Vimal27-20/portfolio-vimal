# Portfolio home + case studies

Scope: the whole site (home `/` and case studies `/work/:slug`). Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads).
Audience and job: recruiters and hiring managers in Ireland skimming for a UX Engineer; success is a résumé download or contact. Proof: the 7 projects, VISE first; real client testimonials; the experience journey. Constraints: all copy and facts from PRODUCT.md; VK logo kept; no invented metrics.
Memorable moment: picking a station on the network map moves the train marker there and the platform sign steps to that project.
Unresolved: exact hero positioning line beyond "UX Engineer".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a transit network: categories are coloured lines, projects are stations with fixed codes, the career is a route. It refuses the category default of an intro hero over a grid of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Pale enamel ground, black enamel sign panels with white Barlow lettering, four functional line colours (Enterprise blue, Mobile red, Web green, IoT amber), circular station roundels with codes, route badges, a departure board in black. One continuous line weight; hierarchy by scale, never colour alone; motion snaps in whole steps.

STORY: The visitor sees the whole body of work as one network in a glance, opens any station to read role, timeline and where it leads, scans the departure board, follows the journey line, reads client notices, and reaches the terminus to email or download the résumé.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Station-name bar (logo, Vimal Kumar, UX Engineer, nav, résumé). Below it the network map fills ~60% width with all 7 stations; the platform sign (~40%) shows VISE (You are here) with image, code, role, timeline and its primary link. Line key under the map doubles as filters.

FORM: The Line, wayfinding system; candidate 3 of 7 on my ordered list; seed key 4a654103.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
